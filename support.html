FAQ

Is EmeraldLife available now?

Not yet. EmeraldLife is in pre-MVP development for an iOS-first, US-first beta.

Is EmeraldLife a medical app?

No. EmeraldLife is a consumer wellness companion for adults 18+. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.

How can I contact support?

You can email support@myemeraldliving.com.

How can I contact the team for privacy, legal, or general questions?

You can email contact@myemeraldliving.com.

What is the core experience?

The planned V1 loop is companion state, one daily Ritual, AI reflection, optional friends-only encouragement, and Profile trends.
