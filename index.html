Current status

EmeraldLife is currently in development and is not yet publicly released for the App Store.

Consumer wellness only

EmeraldLife is designed for adults 18+ as a consumer wellness companion. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.

Product

Built around one meaningful daily action.

The MVP loop is simple: companion state, one Ritual, AI reflection, optional friend encouragement, and Profile trends. Body Energy, Recovery, Readiness, and Happiness guide the experience without turning Home into a score dashboard.

Features

Modeled on the actual app experience.

Home and Ritual

A calm companion state and one small Ritual give the first session a clear, meaningful action.

AI Companion

Selectable tones support check-ins, Ritual coaching, reflection, and next-step suggestions without medical claims.

What's On

Friends-only Ritual shares, reactions, and encouragement keep support private and low pressure.

Profile trends

Wellness bands, streak history, privacy, language, notifications, and companion tone live in Profile.

Algorithm-backed states

Body Energy, Recovery, Readiness, and Happiness shape the companion state, Ritual pacing, and AI tone.

Launch localization

The app is planned for English, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Italian launch support.
