Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

Emerald Living is currently in development. This Privacy Policy explains how Emerald Living may handle information through this website and future app-related services.

1. Overview

This policy applies to our public website and to future Emerald Living services that reference this policy.

2. Information we may collect

We may receive information you choose to share with us, such as messages sent by email. Like most hosted websites, basic technical information may also be recorded through normal hosting logs.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, improve user experience, support product development, and maintain website reliability.

4. Health and wellness information

Emerald Living is intended for general wellness and lifestyle support. It is not designed to provide medical care or clinical services.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information when reasonably necessary for website operation, legal compliance, or protection of rights.

6. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate for a pre-launch website and evolving app services. No method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

Emerald Living is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address the request.

8. International users

If you access this website from outside your home jurisdiction, your information may be processed in locations where laws differ from your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Emerald Living moves toward public launch. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact contact@myemeraldliving.com.
