Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

EmeraldLife is currently in development. This Privacy Policy explains how EmeraldLife handles information through this website and future app-related services.

1. Overview

This policy applies to the public EmeraldLife website and future EmeraldLife services that reference this policy. The policy may be updated before the public app launch.

2. Information we may collect

We may receive information you choose to share with us, such as email messages. This website may also collect basic technical information through normal hosting logs, such as browser type, requested pages, approximate time of access, and device or network information commonly recorded by hosting providers.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, maintain website reliability, understand product interest, improve user experience, and support responsible product development.

4. Health and wellness information

EmeraldLife is intended for general wellness and lifestyle support. Future app-related services may involve wellness, nutrition, habit, and recovery-related information, but EmeraldLife is not designed to provide medical care or clinical services.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to user requests, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or work with service providers that support website or future app operations.

6. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate for a pre-launch website and evolving app services. No method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

EmeraldLife is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address the request.

8. International users

If you access this website from outside your home jurisdiction, information may be processed in locations where privacy laws differ from those in your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as EmeraldLife moves toward public launch. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, contact contact@myemeraldlife.com.
